HSK 3.0 lists
-------------
Sources:
- https://github.com/ivankra/hsk30 (the files used by this project)
- https://github.com/elkmovie/hsk30 (OCR data used by ivankra/hsk30)
- https://github.com/shawkynasr/HSK-official-Query-System (pinyin and part of
  speech data used by ivankra/hsk30)
License: MIT (repository data). The underlying HSK 3.0 standard is published
by the Chinese Ministry of Education.

Word frequency
--------------

2018.
License: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International for the
content. The repository's code is MIT licensed.

Pinyin model
------------
Source: pinyin-input-method-engine
(https://gitee.com/tiger-with-wings-org/pinyin-input-method-engine), a
TypeScript pinyin input-method engine.
License: MIT.
The DAG dictionaries served from public/data/pinyin (dag_char.json and
dag_phrase.json) are the verbatim data files from Pinyin2Hanzi by Letian Sun
(https://github.com/letiantian/Pinyin2Hanzi), also MIT licensed. Both license
texts are in public/licenses/PINYIN-ENGINE.txt.
